## ⭐ Featured Projects

### 📡 Cash Radar — spotting a cash shortage before it happens
`Competition project` · `Oct 2026` · `Python` · `pandas` · `NumPy` · `Streamlit` · `Plotly` · `Excel`

*Best Case Study Competition 2026 (Fintech Recruits) — case set by **Financial Navigator by A352**, a Luxembourg fintech.*

**Problem:** a company can be profitable and still run out of cash, because customers pay late while salaries, taxes and
suppliers are paid on fixed dates. I designed a feature that forecasts cash week by week for 13 weeks and warns the
finance manager before it falls too low.

- **Built a realistic dataset** for a fictional French SME: 18 months of invoices, bills, payroll and bank transactions,
  based on French payment rules and Banque de France statistics
- **Forecast engine in Python** that predicts when each customer will pay from their own payment history, with a
  **Monte Carlo simulation** (4,000 runs) to show a likely range instead of one number
- **Backtested on 66 past forecasts:** average error **€77k vs €304k** for a forecast that assumes on-time payment (**−75%**)
- **Early warning:** if the biggest customer pays 45 days late, cash drops to −€66k; the tool flags it **7 weeks ahead**,
  explains the cause and tests actions, while **a person always makes the final decision**
- **Interactive Streamlit app** where users change delays, test CFO actions or upload their own data;
  plus an analysis notebook, automated tests and a 3-page proposal

🔗 [View Repository](https://github.com/Manibalan28/cash-radar) · 🚀 [Live App]((https://cash-radar-hnlgthgbuorkeuja5fjwbr.streamlit.app/)) · 📄 [Proposal (PDF)](https://github.com/Manibalan28/cash-radar/blob/main/reports/Cash_Radar_Proposal_Financial_Navigator.pdf)

### 🛒 E-Commerce Revenue & Customer Retention Analysis
`Individual project` · `Oct 2026` · `MySQL`

Analyzed e-commerce transaction data to evaluate revenue performance, purchasing behavior and customer retention.

- **Business KPIs:** £10.64M total revenue across 19,960 orders, with an average order value of £533.17
- **New vs. returning customers:** returning customers generated ~**7.28×** more revenue per customer than new customers
- **Cohort retention analysis** to track repeat-purchase behavior over time
- **RFM segmentation** to group customers and surface retention and re-engagement opportunities

**Outcome:** a data-backed view of where retention efforts can lift repeat purchases and customer lifetime value.

🔗 [View Repository](https://github.com/Manibalan28/ecommerce-revenue-retention-analysis)

### 🔐 Global Cyberattack Trends & Financial Impact Dashboard
`Team lead` · `Mar – Apr 2025` · `Tableau`

Led the development of an interactive Tableau dashboard analyzing 15,000+ cybersecurity incidents from 2015 to 2024.

- Visualized attack frequency, financial losses (over **$17B annually**) and incident resolution times
- Identified **Information Technology** as the highest-risk sector
