## Featured Projects

### 🛒 E-Commerce Revenue & Customer Retention Analysis
`Individual Project` · `Oct 2026` · `MySQL`

Analyzed e-commerce transaction data to evaluate revenue performance, purchasing behavior, and customer retention.

- **Business KPIs:** £10.64M total revenue across 19,960 orders, with an average order value of £533.17
- **New vs. returning customers:** returning customers generated ~**7.28×** more revenue per customer than new customers
- **Cohort retention analysis** to track repeat-purchase behavior over time
- **RFM segmentation** to group customers and surface retention and re-engagement opportunities

**Outcome:** a data-backed view of where retention efforts can lift repeat purchases and customer lifetime value.

🔗 [View Repository](https://github.com/Manibalan28/ecommerce-revenue-retention-analysis)

### 🔐 Global Cyberattack Trends & Financial Impact Dashboard
`Team Lead` · `Mar – Apr 2025` · `Tableau`

Led the development of an interactive Tableau dashboard analyzing 15,000+ cybersecurity incidents from 2015 to 2024.

- Visualized attack frequency, financial losses (over **$17B annually**), and incident resolution times
- Identified **Information Technology** as the highest-risk sector
- Found that **32%** of major breaches were linked to weak passwords and zero-day exploits
